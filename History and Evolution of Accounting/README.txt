HISTORY & EVOLUTION OF ACCOUNTING FLASHCARDS

Contents:
- index.html: flashcard interface
- style.css: styling and responsive layout
- script.js: 36 key-point flashcards and interactions

Features:
- Flip cards to reveal answers
- Search by keyword
- Filter by topic
- Shuffle and restart
- Mark cards as known
- Keyboard controls: Space to flip, Left/Right arrows to navigate

How to use:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. No installation or server is required.

The deck focuses on the most important points from the provided
“History and Evolution of Accounting” document, preserving its terminology
and key dates while keeping answers concise.
